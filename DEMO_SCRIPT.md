# MAHA-SETU Phase 2 Demo Script Additions

This extends the core storyline to highlight Phase 2's "Inclusion, Explainability, Simulation, and Officer Intelligence".

## Step 13: The "What-If" Simulation & Cost of Delay
* **Action**: Entrepreneur opens the What-If Simulator from the roadmap.
* **Talk Track**: "What if we increase our investment and hire more employees? Watch the roadmap dynamically adjust via the Rules Registry."
* **Action**: Tweak the employee slider > 50. See new approvals animate in with a green 'New' tag. The Cost-of-Delay calculator updates in real-time, showing potential financial impacts of SLA overruns.

## Step 14: Explainable AI & Transparency
* **Action**: Click "Why am I seeing this?" on the newly added approval.
* **Talk Track**: "AI in government requires absolute transparency. The Explainable-AI Panel shows exactly which rule triggered this, based on which inputs, and its verification status."

## Step 15: Inclusion (Multilingual & Voice)
* **Action**: Toggle language to Marathi, then use the Voice mic in Mitra AI.
* **Talk Track**: "To be truly inclusive, the entire OS switches context. Mitra AI understands Marathi voice prompts and responds in the selected language, breaking down literacy barriers."

## Step 16: The Officer Copilot
* **Action**: Switch to the Officer view (via Consultant/Client switcher).
* **Talk Track**: "Officers are overwhelmed. The Copilot auto-generates a one-click case summary and drafts query letters based on detected inconsistencies."
* **Action**: Click "Generate Query". The Copilot drafts a letter about a missing site plan.

## Step 17: Consent Ledger & Integration
* **Action**: Open Settings -> Privacy & Consent.
* **Talk Track**: "Data minimization is key. The entrepreneur explicitly manages which department can access which document via DigiLocker adapters."

## Step 18: QR-Verifiable Certificates
* **Action**: Officer approves the application. Generate the certificate. Scan the QR code.
* **Talk Track**: "Once approved, a tamper-proof, QR-verifiable certificate is generated. Scanning it opens a public verification page instantly, eliminating forged documents."

## Step 19: The Live Demo Control Panel
* **Action**: Press Ctrl+Shift+D to open the hidden Demo Panel.
* **Talk Track**: "For hackathon judges, we built a time machine."
* **Action**: Click "Advance SLA by 7 days". Watch predictive delay flags turn red, WhatsApp channel simulate alerts, and the dashboard adapt.
